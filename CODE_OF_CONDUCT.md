# Code of Conduct

AI 3D Studio adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of
conduct: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people
of every background and experience level, and keep discussions focused on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
tolerated in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at the contact
address listed in the repository's README or security policy. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) according to
the severity and pattern of the behaviour.
